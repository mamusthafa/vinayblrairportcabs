What does the 50 KM Range Cover?
Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) is located in Devanahalli, North Bengaluru. The 50 KM range covers over 85% of Bengaluru localities—including Hebbal, Yelahanka, Sahakar Nagar, Indiranagar, MG Road, Whitefield, Koramangala, HSR Layout, Banashankari, and Jayanagar. Suburbs beyond 50 KM (such as Electronic City Phase 2 or Jigani) are charged the 50 KM base fare plus nominal extra kilometers.
